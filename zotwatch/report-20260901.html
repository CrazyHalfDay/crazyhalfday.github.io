Significant variability in marine hydrate stability

海洋水合物稳定性的显著变异性

2026-08-31
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Aditya Kumar，Ann E. Cook，Alexey Portnov，Alejandro Cardona，Jess I. T. Hillman

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04005-w

“Bottom simulating reflections (BSR) vary over 100 s of meters and do not coincide with the calculated thermodynamic base of methane hydrate stability, as shown using 70 BSR measurements across the northern Gulf of Mexico.” Natural gas hydrate stores hundreds to thousands of gigatons of metastable carbon on Earth in a solid, ice-like clathrate. Hydrate stability is well defined in laboratory measurements, but we argue that fundamental constraints on hydrate stability are not understood in natural systems. In marine seismic data, bottom simulating reflections (BSR) are thought to mark the base of the hydrate stability zone. BSRs usually parallel the seafloor and deepen in the sediment column as water depths increase. Herein, we observe that BSR depths from 70 BSRs across the northern Gulf of Mexico vary over 100 s of meters and do not coincide with the calculated thermodynamic base of methane hydrate stability. BSR depths also do not have a strong correlation with water depth. Moreover, at several locations in deepwater, BSRs approach the seafloor suggesting hydrate here could be at risk of dissociation. Subsurface salt appears to control broad trends in BSR depths in the Gulf of Mexico, but there are still unaccounted processes influencing the stability of hydrate systems.

A dual-mineral protection mechanism for organic matter preservation and its implications for Mars habitability and biosignature detection

有机质保存的双矿物保护机制及其对火星宜居性和生物特征检测的意义

2026-08-29
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Tongtong Huang，Jianxun Shen，Li Liu，Jialong Hao，Yan Chen 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03992-0

Understanding organic matter preservation in extreme environments is crucial for detecting potential biosignatures on Mars. Although clay minerals and evaporites can each preserve organic matter individually, their synergistic preservation mechanisms remain an active area of investigation. Here, we investigate the preservation of organic matter in ancient evaporite minerals from the Qaidam Basin, using integrated in situ microanalytical techniques to analyze solid/fluid inclusions within displacive gypsum and cumulate halite formed during the Middle Pleistocene. Our analyses reveal well-preserved organic matter, including β-carotene and kerogen, adsorbed onto clay particles both in solid inclusions within gypsum and fluid inclusions within halite. Geochemical data are consistent with a potential biogenic contribution, whereas metagenomic data from modern Qaidam soils provide ecological context rather than direct evidence for the origin of the preserved organic matter. We propose a dual-mineral protection mechanism, wherein clay minerals initially adsorb organic compounds, which are subsequently encapsulated by growing salt crystals. This two-stage process leverages the complementary protective properties of both mineral types and enhances long-term organic preservation. Our findings identify a specific mineral association capable of preserving Martian organic biosignatures and provide strategic guidance for selecting exploration targets in future Mars missions. A two-step interaction between clay minerals and evaporite salts may promote organic matter preservation, with possible relevance to Mars, according to microanalytical analysis of the Middle Pleistocene gypsum and halite minerals from China’s western Qaidam Basin.

Metasomatic dehydration of talc constrains the maximum depth of slab–mantle decoupling in subduction zones

2026-08-29
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Nanfei Cheng，I-Ming Chou，Zhenhao Duan，Zhou Gui，Haiyan Zhang 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03979-x

Abstract Talc largely contributes to water transport into Earth’s interior in subduction zones and lubrication of the slab–mantle interface. However, its maximum stability limit has typically been evaluated under silica-saturated conditions. In-situ high-pressure, high-temperature experiments up to 33.8 kbar and 875 °C reveal that in water-rich, low-silica environments, geologically evidenced by talc coexisting with antigorite and olivine at natural subduction interfaces, talc undergoes a metasomatic dehydration reaction that produces forsterite and a silica-bearing aqueous fluid. This reaction shifts the dehydration boundary to ≈ 705 °C at 10 kbar and ≈ 650 °C at 30 kbar in the experimental low-silica condition, that is 90–170 °C lower than that under silica-saturated conditions. Critically, this metasomatic boundary intersects slab thermal profiles at a depth of ≈ 80 km across diverse global subduction zones, from cold (e.g., Mariana) to hot (e.g., Cascadia), matching the maximum depth of decoupling between the subducting slab and mantle wedge, which separates the stagnant, cold forearc mantle from the flowing, hot arc mantle.

A nutrient control on oxygenation dynamics during Earth’s Great Oxidation Episode

Nature Communications (IF: 14.7)

Lewis J. Alcott，Benjamin J. W. Mills，Andrey Bekker，Zidong Peng，Simon W. Poulton

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-76597-y

Abstract Earth’s first rise in atmospheric oxygen occurred over a protracted period from ca. 2.43 to 2.06 billion years ago during the Great Oxidation Episode (GOE). The GOE was marked by extreme climatic instability, which likely impacted the bioavailability of the principal limiting nutrient, phosphorus. Here, we investigate phosphorus cycling across the final two glaciations of the GOE, as documented by the Transvaal Supergroup, South Africa. Utilising a phosphorus phase partitioning approach, we show that following an enhanced weathering-induced phosphorus influx that stimulated oxygenation in the aftermath of glaciation, sedimentary phosphorus recycling was controlled by feedback-driven fluctuations in sulphate availability and oceanic redox state. Biogeochemical modelling suggests that this dynamic interplay drove major instability in atmospheric oxygen levels during Earth’s protracted transition to a persistently oxygenated atmosphere.

DOI: 10.1126/science.aed9893

Foreshock activity is a key consideration in traffic-light protocol (TLP) to mitigate seismic risk from injection-induced earthquakes (IIEs). However, the seismogenic processes of IIE foreshocks remain poorly understood. Using an enhanced western Canada catalog (2014–2024), we analyzed 77 IIE sequences to statistically delineate foreshock patterns before local magnitude ( M L ) ≥ 3 mainshocks. We found that 92% are preceded by foreshocks and that foreshock productivity and spatiotemporal patterns reflect the interplay among fluid injection, the seismogenic index, and the fault stress state. Sequence-specific analyses elucidate three nucleation models: fluid-driven preslip with weakened source asperity, fluid-driven preslip with intact source asperity, and fluid-driven cascade, highlighting the central role of fluids in enabling aseismic slip and interevent stress transfer before mainshock rupture. These results imply that IIE monitoring strategies should be spatially conditioned according to foreshock productivity.

Reversed marine phosphorus gradient in the Ediacaran Ocean

埃迪卡拉纪海洋中反转的海洋磷梯度

2026-08-27
Nature Communications (IF: 14.7)

Matthew S. Dodd，Chao Li，Meng Cheng，Jun Hu，Zihu Zhang 等

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-77093-z

Carbonate associated phosphate indicates that Ediacaran oceans had more P in shallow waters but less in the deep sea, opposite to modern. A distal P shortage may have aided deep-water oxygenation and early animals. The Ediacaran Period (635–539 Ma) witnessed the emergence of early animals, rapid oceanic oxygenation, and rising oceanic P availability. However, the relationships among these developments remain intensely debated. Here, we present high-resolution carbonate-associated phosphate (CAP) data from seven Ediacaran sections spanning diverse depositional environments across South China. Contrary to the modern ocean, our measurements reveal an inverted phosphorus (P) gradient, with lower P availability at depth. By integrating these data with a quantitative biogeochemical model, we show that increasing shallow-water P inputs drove enhanced productivity and bottom-water anoxia on continental shelves, while P-starved distal oceans became progressively oxygenated from the top downward, providing an oxygenated niche—a deep-ocean cradle—for early metazoan evolution. Our study establishes a conceptual framework linking nutrient dynamics, marine oxygenation, and evolutionary innovation in Earth’s early history.

DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aeg0341

Nickel isotopes (δ 60 Ni) are an emerging tool for understanding ocean biogeochemical evolution, given improved modern Ni budget constraints. Here, we provide constraints on past ocean Ni isotope compositions from organic-rich sediments from Tarfaya Basin core SN°4. The data constrain Late Cretaceous ocean δ 60 Ni to ∼+0.8 to +1.0 per mil (‰), lower than the modern value of +1.34‰. DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03984-0

“Previously unrecognized ca. 700–694 Ma Olumi–Sumbi large igneous province with low‑latitude emplacement may have contributed to both initiating and sustaining Earth's longest glaciation, the Sturtian glaciation, shown using baddeleyite uranium-lead ages and paleomagnetic constraints.” The Sturtian Snowball Earth cryochron (ca. 717–660 Ma) was the Earth’s longest glaciation, yet mechanisms for its persistence remain debated. We report high‑precision ID‑TIMS baddeleyite U–Pb ages of 700.6 ± 9.5 Ma and 694.0 ± 2.0 Ma for the Olumi dolerite dyke swarm (Gabon) and Sumbi dolerite sills (DRC), defining a previously unrecognized ca. 700–694 Ma Olumi–Sumbi large igneous province (LIP). Paleomagnetic constraints for the Olumi dyke indicate low‑latitude emplacement, placing the Congo Craton near the equator. Together with the ca. 719 Ma Franklin LIP, these tropical LIPs may have contributed to both initiating and sustaining the Sturtian glaciation. Episodic magmatism might have driven transient warming and deglaciation via volcanic CO_2 emissions, followed by efficient chemical weathering of fresh mafic rocks, which enhanced CO_2 drawdown and restored global ice cover. This suggests that tropical emplacement of LIPs during the Sturtian Snowball Earth cryochron might have strongly impacted the dynamics of the Earth’s longest glaciation.

2026-08-26
Journal of Geochemical Exploration (IF: 3.3)

Ming Liang，Chao Wu，Anpeng Qiu，Min Guo，Xunhu He

DOI: 10.1016/j.gexplo.2026.108202

The Jinmen pluton is situated on the southern margin of the Nanling metallogenic belt, where a skarn-type Cu-Fe-Mo-Au polymetallic deposit has been discovered in its proximity. Skarn-type deposits are widely recognized as an important component of porphyry metallogenic systems; however, systematic studies on the emplacement age, petrological classification, genetic relationship with skarn mineralization, and porphyry metallogenic potential of this pluton remain lacking. This study presents an integrated dataset of whole-rock geochemistry, zircon U-Pb-Hf isotopes, and molybdenite ReOs geochronology for the Jinmen pluton, which consists of biotite monzogranite with subordinate granite dikes and mafic microgranular enclaves (MMEs). Zircon LA-ICP-MS UPb dating yields weighted mean ages of 161.6 ± 0.6 Ma to 158.7 ± 0.6 Ma for biotite monzogranite, 161.1 ± 0.8 Ma for granite dike, and 161.0 ± 0.6 Ma for MMEs, indicating coeval emplacement. Four molybdenite samples yield a weighted average ReOs age of 162.0 ± 0.4 Ma, confirming synchronous magmatism and mineralization during the Late Jurassic. All zircons yield negative Hf isotopic compositions (εHf(t) = −7.17 to −1.69), with two-stage model ages corresponding to the Paleoproterozoic (1.32–1.66 Ga). Proterozoic (1680 ± 30 Ma) and Silurian (438 ± 13 Ma) inherited zircons were identified in the biotite monzogranite, suggesting possible partial melting of ancient crustal materials. The development of acicular apatite in MMEs and oscillatory zoning in plagioclase within the host granite indicates magma mixing. Combined with whole-rock geochemical constraints, these characteristics demonstrate that the Jinmen pluton is an I-type granite originating from crustal anatexis modified by mantle material input; the MMEs are interpreted as the products of magma mixing between enriched mantle and ancient crustal melts. The biotite monzogranite additionally exhibits high oxygen fugacity (ΔFMQ = +0.9 to +3.4) and elevated V/Sc ratios (an indicator of a water-rich magma), which favors the exsolution of ore-forming fluids. Furthermore, a negative correlation between ΔFMQ and log[104 × (Eu/Eu*)/YbN] reflects magmatic oxidation during ascent, a process that enhances copper mobility and enrichment. These geochemical fingerprints are comparable to those of major porphyry Cu deposits worldwide, indirectly indicating the porphyry Cu exploration potential of the Jinmen pluton.

Variations of F-Cl-Br-I in the Pinghe alkaline complex (South China): Constraints on halogen budgets of the metasomatized lithospheric mantle

2026-08-25
Chemical Geology (IF: 3.6)

Dan-Zhen Ma，Lian-Xun Wang，Sebastian Tappe，Yu-Xiang Zhu，Zhen-Bing She 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.chemgeo.2026.123674

The subcontinental lithospheric mantle (SCLM), modified by subducted oceanic components, acts as a major reservoir for deep-Earth halogens. This study investigates the halogen geochemistry (F, Cl, Br, I) of the alkaline rocks from Pinghe (South China) to constrain halogen behaviour during mantle metasomatism and subsequent magmatic-to-hydrothermal processes. Bulk-rock geochemical trends demonstrate that primitive lithologies (jacupirangite and ijolite) are characterized by high F (up to 2449 ppm) but relatively low Cl and Br contents. Conversely, more evolved lithologies (sodalite urtite and syenites) exhibit pronounced enrichment in Cl (up to 1253 ppm) and Br (up to 2.53 ppm). Mineral compositional analyses and element mapping reveal that fluorapatite is the primary F-host in jacupirangite and syenite, whereas biotite dominates the F-budget in ijolite and sodalite urtite. In contrast, Cl is partitioned into amphibole (jacupirangite and amphibole syenite), biotite (ijolite and nepheline syenite), and sodalite (urtite). Notably, the F contents of apatite decrease from jacupirangite to ijolite but increase in the most evolved suite, showing an antithetic relationship with Cl. This apparent decoupling is driven by extensive fractionation of fluorapatite, which sequesters F into cumulates and drives the residual melts toward progressive enrichment in Cl and Br, a signature recorded by the late-stage crystallization of Cl-rich amphibole and biotite. Despite the coupled behaviour of Cl and Br (mean Cl/Br of 540, excluding outliers), the F/Cl ratios vary by over three orders of magnitude due to the competing effects of early fluorapatite fractionation and late-stage sodalite crystallization. The combination of elevated halogen/Nb ratios (e.g., Cl/Nb up to 894), robust positive correlations between Ba/Nb and halogen/Nb, and distinctly low Br/Cl and I/Cl, indicates that the SCLM had been metasomatized by aqueous fluids derived from subducted altered oceanic crust and serpentinite. This study highlights that extreme halogen fractionation can occur during alkaline magma evolution, and our findings suggests that the least-evolved peralkaline melts carry a halogen signature inherited from a continental lithospheric mantle source pervasively metasomatized by slab-derived fluids.

Sulfur diffusion in lunar melts and its implications for lunar mantle sulfur abundance

月球熔体中的硫扩散及其对月球地幔硫丰度的指示

2026-08-29
Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta (IF: 4.5)

Kang Liu，Li Zhang，Hesan Wu，Wan-Cai Li，Huaiwei Ni

DOI: 10.1016/j.gca.2026.08.028

Lunar volcanic glass beads preserve records of sulfur evolution in primitive lunar magmas and provide important constraints on the sulfur inventory of the lunar interior. Reconstruction of their pre-eruptive sulfur contents requires sulfur diffusivity data for lunar melts, which have remained largely unavailable. Here we experimentally determined sulfur diffusivities in three synthetic lunar basaltic melts, spanning compositions from Apollo green (low TiO2, 0.52 wt%) and orange (intermediate TiO2, 8.58 wt%) to red (high TiO2, 13.93 wt%), with SiO2 decreasing from 45.35 to 35.92 wt% and MgO from 16.00 to 11.00 wt%, using diffusion-couple experiments at 0.5 GPa and 1674–1876 K in a piston cylinder apparatus. Sulfur diffusivity increases systematically from green to orange to red glass compositions, with NBO/T rising from 1.76 to 2.49 to 2.82, respectively. At 1673 K, sulfur diffusivity in lunar melts is 6–13 times higher than that in terrestrial basaltic melts (NBO/T = 0.61–0.79), primarily owing to the more depolymerized structure of lunar melts. Combining these results with published experimental data, we develop a general parameterization for sulfur diffusivity in anhydrous lunar and terrestrial silicate melts under reduced conditions as a function of temperature (T) and melt composition (X Si+Al, expressed by the combined mole fraction of Si + Al), which reproduces nearly all available experimental data within a factor of 2. Applying our new sulfur diffusivity data to Apollo green and orange glass beads yields revised pre-eruptive sulfur concentrations of 282–311 and 331 μg/g, corresponding to mantle source sulfur abundances of 9–28 and 27–63 μg/g, respectively. These estimates differ substantially from values previous inferred for Apollo and Chang’e mare basalts, indicating significant sulfur heterogeneity within the lunar interior, likely inherited from lunar magma ocean differentiation and subsequent mantle overturn.

Jiashuo Liu，Bo Wang，Charles Gumiaux，Liangshu Shu，Hugues Raimbourg 等

DOI: 10.1130/b39328.1

The way in which partial melting weakens the middle continental crust and triggers lateral flow is fundamental to understanding orogenic processes, yet the specific roles of magmatic heat and deformation remain debated. We addressed this issue through an integrated study of the early Mesozoic granitoids and migmatites in the Yunkai massif (South China). To better understand the early Mesozoic crustal evolution and geodynamic processes of South China, we conducted detailed studies on structural geology, pressure-temperature (P-T) estimates, and multimineral geochronology. The main deformation structure is represented by regional NE-SW stretching that was associated with widespread magmatism and partial melting under amphibolite-facies metamorphism. The strain partitioning at different locations shows dome-like structures, involving horizontal stretching at Fuhu Hill and central Hailing Island, subvertical shearing along the coast of Hailing Island, and dextral strike-slip shearing in the Jiahai area. Phase equilibrium modeling on high-grade metamorphic rocks defines peak P-T conditions at 6.8−7.0 kbar and 780−810 °C, indicating midcrustal high-temperature metamorphism dated between ca. 244 Ma and 232 Ma. The published data and our new results document that the high-temperature metamorphism occurred ∼10 m.y. later than magmatism dated between ca. 254 Ma and 234 Ma, indicating a magma-induced cause of the metamorphism. This midcrustal melting and high-temperature metamorphism were likely a consequence of either Indochina−South China collision or possible Paleo-Pacific subduction beneath the SE China continent. Our study provides a new magma-induced model for intracontinental partial melting and midcrustal flow, with broad implications for understanding crustal anatexis and rheology in orogens worldwide.

Abstract Late Archean sanukitoid rocks preserve the key to unravel the transitional crust–mantle geodynamics of the early Earth. The increasingly identified chemical diversity of Archean sanukitoid rocks could provide new clues to clarify this issue. Here, a suite of late Neoarchean (~2560–2491 Ma) dioritic and (quartz) monzodioritic gneisses were identified in the Pingquan complex of North China Craton, and whole-rock major and trace element as well as zircon U–Pb and Lu-Hf isotopic data were obtained for these rocks. In particular, the (quartz) monzodioritic gneisses show higher K2O/Na2O, Sr + Ba, Sr/Y, and more enriched zircon εHf(t2) values than the dioritic gneisses, and their overall chemical variation broadly overlaps with that of global sanukitoid rocks. Petrogenetic and thermodynamic-trace element modeling studies were carried out. The dioritic gneisses were modeled to be derived from partial melting of a mixed mantle source (65% depleted MORB mantle (DMM), 29% altered oceanic crust melts (OCM), and 6% sediment-derived melts (SM)) at 1.4–1.9 GPa (1030–1060°C and 2 wt.% H2O content). The monzodioritic gneisses require more sediment recycling (9%), and were derived from the metasomatized mantle sources (~65% DMM, ~26% OCM, and ~ 9% SM) at higher pressures of ~ 2.0–2.1 GPa (980–1050°C and 2 wt.% H2O content). In comparison, the quartz monzodioritic gneisses were derived from the metasomatized mantle sources (~60% DMM, ~29% OCM, and ~ 11% SM) at the highest pressures of ~ 2.4–2.8 GPa (1050–1070°C and 2 wt.% H2O content). Integrating the modeled melting P–T conditions and regional geological data, these rocks formed most likely under a hot plate subduction system. Further compilation of global Archean sanukitoid rocks indicate that similar chemical diversity as the Pingquan case appeared globally during the Neoarchean. This chemical diversity cannot be achieved without sediment recycling, which is coincident with the inferred late Archean continental emergence and weathering, and likely evolved under the plate tectonic paradigm.

Subduction-driven destruction and reconfiguration of the South China lithosphere: evidence from shear-wave velocity structure

Minling Wang，Xin Chen，Keith Priestley，Xiaofeng Liang

DOI: 10.1016/j.gr.2026.08.008

The South China continent records a complex history of lithospheric modification related to supercontinent cycles, Paleo-Pacific plate subduction, and intracontinental deformation linked to the growth of the Tibetan Plateau. However, the spatial pattern of lithospheric structure and the mechanisms responsible for Mesozoic–Cenozoic lithospheric reworking remain debated. Here we present a high-resolution shear-wave velocity model of the crust and upper mantle beneath South China derived from teleseismic two-plane-wave surface-wave tomography. By jointly inverting phase and amplitude measurements using finite-frequency sensitivity kernels, we resolve detailed lateral variations in lithospheric structure across the Yangtze and Cathaysia blocks. The results reveal pronounced east–west lithospheric heterogeneity. The western Yangtze Block, particularly beneath the Sichuan Basin, preserves a thick high-velocity lithospheric root (>200 km), consistent with the survival of an ancient cratonic keel. In contrast, the lithosphere beneath the Cathaysia Block and southeastern Yangtze is strongly thinned (∼60–100 km) and characterized by extensive low-velocity anomalies within the crust and uppermost mantle, indicating substantial lithospheric modification and magmatic reworking. Along the eastern margin of the Tibetan Plateau, prominent velocity gradients and mid–lower crustal low-velocity zones suggest distributed ductile deformation and possible channel flow within the crust. These results indicate that the present lithospheric architecture of South China reflects a two-stage geodynamic evolution involving Mesozoic lithospheric thinning driven by rollback of the Paleo-Pacific plate, followed by Cenozoic intracontinental deformation associated with outward growth of the Tibetan Plateau. Our findings provide new constraints on the mechanisms of lithospheric destruction, continental reworking, and tectonic coupling between eastern Asia and the Tibetan Plateau.

Identifying coseismic deformation along shallow faults is crucial for assessing seismic hazards associated with surface rupture and ground shaking, yet unequivocal evidence of seismic slip in carbonate-hosted faults remains uncertain. Active normal faults on Miyako Island in the southern Ryukyu arc (Japan) are hosted in Middle to Late Pleistocene carbonate rocks derived from uplifted coral reefs and are considered capable of generating magnitude-7-class earthquakes. Field observations of one of these faults show that the ∼50-m-thick fault zone contains multiple ∼2-mm-thick narrow slip zones characterized by mirror-like, striated slip surfaces and limestone fragments embedded in an ultrafine-grained calcite matrix. Microstructural analyses indicate that calcite grains within these zones experienced localized dislocation creep and recrystallization at temperatures &gt;400 °C, substantially higher than the ambient temperature of ∼25 °C. These microstructural features provide compelling evidence of coseismic frictional heating at shallow crustal levels, providing key constraints on paleoseismicity and the seismic hazards of carbonate-hosted faults.

This study integrates syn-ore sericite in-situ Rb–Sr geochronology, quartz in-situ SIMS oxygen isotopes, fluid inclusions microthermometry, and SEM–EDS to constrain the timing and fluid evolution of the Kongquegoubei lode-gold deposit in the Kumishi metallogenic belt, eastern South Tianshan. 研究结果：研究确定了四个变质阶段：进变质（M1）、峰期（M2，~760–805°C/8–9.2 kbar）、减压（M3，~760–810°C/6.3 kbar）和冷却（M4），并识别出三个独居石变质生长世代（~1.93、~1.90、~1.87 Ga）。这些结果揭示了一条长期的顺时针P–T–t轨迹，反映了JLJOB从碰撞增厚到折返剥露的古元古代造山演化过程。区域对比表明，JLJOB内麻粒岩经历了相似的变质-构造历史，而峰期条件的差异主要受控于暴露构造层次和保存偏差。

局限性：研究主要依赖相平衡模拟和矿物组合推断，对熔体参与和流体行为可能未完全约束；独居石年代学解释存在多期生长与铅丢失的潜在不确定性。此外，单个剖面的P–T–t轨迹能否代表整个造山带的演化仍需更多空间数据支持。

未来方向：未来可对JLJOB不同构造层次的麻粒岩开展系统的变质年代学与热力学模拟，结合热-力学数值模型定量模拟碰撞-折返过程；也可利用石榴石微量元素扩散剖面或多种同位素体系（如Lu–Hf、Sm–Nd）进一步约束冷却速率和构造抬升时限。

研究相关性：该论文为研究前寒武纪造山带变质演化、顺时针P–T–t轨迹与碰撞-折返过程的研究者提供了详细实例，尤其对理解华北克拉通古元古代构造热事件及含堇青石泥质麻粒岩的相平衡模拟方法具有重要参考价值。
