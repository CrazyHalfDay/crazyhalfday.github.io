This study links higher diamond grades in kimberlites to water-rich olivine. Modeling suggests that rapid magma ascent preserves higher olivine water contents and facilitates efficient diamond transport, highlighting ascent rate as a key control on diamond preservation. Kimberlites are volatile-rich magmas that transport diamonds from the deep lithosphere to the surface, yet the exact processes controlling their variable diamond abundances remain debated. Compiled dataset of water contents in mantle olivine shows that kimberlites containing water-rich (>40 wt. ppm) olivine exhibit higher diamond grades. Diffusion modeling coupled with estimates of the ascent velocities of olivine xenocrysts indicate that preserved water in the cores of olivine crystals reflects variable magma ascent rates. The link between olivine water content and diamond grade highlights that ascent rate is a key factor in controlling the varied diamond abundance in kimberlites. Water in olivine thus provides an additional, complementary, and cost-effective proxy for estimating kimberlite ascent and diamond-carrying efficiency.

2026-10-21
Science Advances (IF: 11.7)

Zaicong Wang，Xiang Wang，Haijin Xu，Junbo Zhang，Frances Elaine Jenner 等

DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aeh0254

Subduction recycles surface materials into the mantle and shapes its chemical evolution. Whether continental subduction leaves chalcophile-element imprints in the mantle that differ from those of oceanic subduction remains poorly understood. Here, we show that primitive basaltic magmas from the North China Craton exhibit pronounced copper depletion, silver enrichment, and low copper/silver (200 to 1200), together with continental crust–like trace element patterns and radiogenic-isotope compositions. These features contrast sharply with those of the convective mantle, mid-ocean ridge basalts, and oceanic arc basalts, and reflect chemical signatures inherited from subducted continental crust and retained over geological timescales in metasomatized domains of a relatively cold lithospheric mantle. Coupled lead enrichment and unradiogenic lead-isotope signatures further identify metasomatized mantle as an important reservoir of Earth’s unradiogenic lead. Our results reveal distinct modes of chalcophile-element recycling and mantle inheritance during continental and oceanic subduction, with broad implications for mantle chemical evolution and the critical-metal fertility of continental lithosphere.

2026-10-21
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (IF: 11.1)

Gerardo Dominguez，Caroline Caplan，Lauren Tafla，Joshua Lucas，Ming-Chang Liu 等

DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2610806123

Mass-independent isotope fractionation (MIF) of oxygen provides key insights into atmospheric chemistry and the origin of isotopic anomalies in planetary materials. To date, MIF has been primarily associated with gas-phase processes involving symmetry-dependent reaction pathways, most notably ozone formation. Whether analogous effects can arise in condensed phases or at interfaces remains an open question. Here we report laboratory experiments demonstrating that energetic particle irradiation of icy oxide surfaces produces mass-independent oxygen isotope fractionation at temperatures as low as 10 K. Electron irradiation of SiO 2 and Al 2 O 3 substrates in the presence of H 2 O ice results in measurable shifts in Δ 17 O in the underlying solids. The resulting three-isotope relationships include slope-1-like behavior in SiO 2 and Al 2 O 3 and distinct, substrate-dependent trends in Al 2 O 3 , indicating that irradiation-induced defect formation and interfacial chemistry govern the isotope effects. The magnitude of the observed anomalies demonstrates that irradiation-driven processes can generate substantial oxygen isotope heterogeneity in condensed phases. These results identify a previously unrecognized class of MIF and provide a quantitative framework for interpreting oxygen isotope signatures in radiation-processed environments, including interstellar grains and early solar system materials.

Metamorphic proteins challenge the single-sequence-structure-function paradigm by switching between distinct folds that often adopt complementary functions. Using multinuclear chemical exchange saturation transfer and relaxation dispersion NMR, combined with chemical shift–based structure determination and protein engineering, we visualized the atomic resolution structure of an “invisible” state of the metamorphic chemokine lymphotactin. The stabilization of such hybrid intermediates is likely to have facilitated the evolution of fold-switching and represents a powerful strategy for protein design.

Effusive sedimentary volcanism experimentally reproduced under Martian-like conditions

2026-10-21
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Ondřej Krýza，Petr Brož，Jacob Adler，Matthew Sylvest，Manish Patel

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04155-x

Abstract Rapid boiling, cooling, and freezing are commonly assumed to rapidly quench metastable liquid eruptions exposed to near-vacuum planetary environments, influencing both sedimentary volcanism on Mars and cryovolcanism on icy worlds. However, how these coupled phase transitions govern eruptive processes remains largely unknown. Here we show experimentally that boiling instead sustains coupled ballistic-effusive mud eruptions under Martian-like pressure conditions (~4.5 mbar). Using an ice-sealed metastable reservoir, we reproduced subsurface-driven eruptions initiated by internally generated boiling-induced overpressure. Eruptions evolved systematically from short-lived ballistic fountains toward episodic effusive emplacement accompanied by coherent mud-flow formation. Ballistic transport redistributed liquid mud around the vent and promoted proximal accumulation that subsequently coalesced into sustained flows despite ongoing cooling and freezing. Numerical modeling further demonstrates that erupted mud would remain largely unfrozen during ballistic transport under Martian gravity conditions. Together, our results show that rapid boiling does not suppress sedimentary volcanism under low-pressure conditions, but instead actively sustains eruptive transport and surface emplacement. These findings provide new constraints on this process on Mars and establish a broader physical framework for metastable liquid eruptions across the Solar System.

Abstract Offshore freshened groundwater (OFG) is a significant yet underexplored component of the global water cycle, and its spatial distribution, particularly its links to past glacial processes, remains poorly constrained. Tracing Orogenic Evolution From Multi‐Detrital Mineral Records: U‐Pb Geochronology and Trace‐Element Constraints From Glacial Diamictites

2026-10-17
Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems (IF: 3.1)

Lijun Wang，Shoufa Lin，Wenjiao Xiao，Cees R. van Staal，Chris R. M. McFarlane 等

DOI: 10.1029/2026gc013024

Abstract Integrating multiple detrital mineral systems provides a powerful framework for reconstructing source‐region tectonothermal evolution because different minerals record complementary aspects of magmatic and metamorphic processes. Glacial diamictites are particularly valuable for this approach because extensive glacial erosion can sample broad source regions and diverse crustal levels while minimizing chemical weathering and preserving unstable mineral phases. Here, we present integrated U‐Pb geochronological and trace‐element analyses of detrital apatite and rutile from Marinoan‐aged glacial diamictites of the Yangtze Block in South China and combine these data with existing zircon data sets. Detrital apatite records dominant mafic and I‐type magmatism between ∼970 and 650 Ma, with more evolved peraluminous signatures appearing at 850–820 Ma, broadly consistent with declining zircon εHf(t) values. Detrital rutile reveals dominant Neoproterozoic (∼970–790 Ma) and subordinate Paleoproterozoic (2.2–1.8 Ga) metamorphic signatures. Zr‐in‐rutile thermometry indicates temperatures of 480°C–980°C, including a distinct ∼950–820 Ma high‐pressure, low‐temperature population consistent with blueschist‐ to eclogite‐facies conditions. These data record major tectonic transitions within the Panxi‐Hannan orogen of the northwestern Yangtze Block, from an intra‐oceanic arc (970–880 Ma) to a continental arc (870–790 Ma), followed by regional extension after 790 Ma. The cessation of rutile and metamorphic apatite ages coincides with the onset of regional extension in the source orogen. The ∼950–820 Ma cold‐subduction signature expands the global record of Tonian low‐geothermal‐gradient metamorphism. Our results demonstrate that integrating multiple detrital mineral systems yields a more complete tectonic history than any single provenance proxy.

Tracing magmatic evolution: Alkali feldspar records of crystal-melt separation and cumulate remelting

2026-10-22
Lithos (IF: 4.5)

Hong-Yang Shi，Xi-Sheng Xu，Yan Xia，Reiner Klemd，Chang-Hong Liang

DOI: 10.1016/j.lithos.2026.108824

Crystal-melt separation and cumulate remelting within subvolcanic silicic magma reservoirs are of great significance for deciphering catastrophic volcanic eruptions, the generation of highly fractionated granites/rhyolites, and volcanic-plutonic relationships. However, the effective identification of crystal-melt separation and cumulate remelting remains a matter of approach. To address this, we present a substantial number of in-situ feldspar major and trace element analyses, as alkali feldspar is usually the dominant mineral involved in mush processes within silicic magma reservoirs, complemented by detailed microstructural observations, TIMA, whole-rock geochemical analyses, and zircon UPb dating and Hf isotope analyses. This study focuses on the Late Cretaceous Daiyunshan volcanic field, SE China, which includes the Shiniushan and Yunshan volcanic-plutonic complexes, as well as the Pucheng quartz monzodiorite. We propose that the Pucheng quartz monzodiorite represents the intermediate magma reservoir of the Shiniushan and Yunshan volcanic-plutonic complexes. The intermediate magma reservoir evolved further into the Yunshan subvolcanic silicic mush through crystal-melt separation dominated by plagioclase and into the Shiniushan subvolcanic silicic mush through crystal-melt separation dominated by both alkali feldspar and plagioclase, respectively. Ba and Sr variation trends, as well as Ti, Mg, Li, Eu, Gd, and light rare earth element characteristics of alkali feldspar from different rock types within a rock series, were used as indicators of crystal-melt separation within the intermediate-felsic mush. For the Shiniushan volcanic-plutonic complex, typical remobilization and cumulate remelting induced by magma recharge occurred within the subvolcanic silicic mush after crystal-melt separation, as reflected by typical and pervasive melt resorption textures of plagioclase, alkali feldspar, quartz, and mafic minerals. During the alkali feldspar-dominated cumulate remelting, the Ba contents of the interstitial matrix alkali feldspar and of the recrystallized rims of the alkali feldspar phenocrysts increased to significantly higher values than those of the cores (Gd shows the same trend owing to amphibole remelting). This remelting process caused an “inverse Ba zoning” pattern in the alkali feldspar phenocrysts. The mush remobilization and cumulate remelting further induced the extrusion of crystal-rich rhyolite. Moreover, the cumulate remelting reduced the phenocryst content (i.e., increased the porosity) of the porphyritic quartz monzonite formed by the residual mush, thereby obscuring its cumulate framework.
