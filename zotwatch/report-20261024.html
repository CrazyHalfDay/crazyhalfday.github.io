Drying and rewetting of riverbed sediments results in cold to neutral biogeochemical moments

2026-10-23
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Vanessa Garayburu-Caruso，Maggi Laan，Laura Coulson，Dillman Delgado，Brieanne Forbes 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04081-y

Abstract Sediment respiration in streams/rivers impacts global biogeochemical cycles, yet is mostly studied under perennially-inundated conditions despite all river corridors having variably inundated areas and most streams going dry each year. Previous experiments observed sediment rewetting to cause elevated or suppressed aerobic respiration, indicating variation across studies. To deepen understanding, we conducted 21-day wet/dry experiments with sediments from 64 streams spread across the contiguous United States. Here we show that drying and rewetting sediments from perennial and non-perennial streams had near-neutral effects or suppressed O 2 consumption rates. These biogeochemical cold moments contrast previous studies that observed hot moments. Sediment texture and microbial activity explained variation in responses to rewetting, but this did not reveal why some studies observed cold moments while others observed hot moments. To solve this we propose a unifying conceptual model whereby hot and cold moments exist simultaneously upon rewetting by considering both dry-state and perennially-inundated reference points.

Local growth laws determine global shape of molluscan shells

2026-10-23
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (IF: 11.1)

Huan Liu，Kaushik Bhattacharya

DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2614478123

Molluscan shells come in various shapes and sizes. Despite this diversity, each species produces a shell with a characteristic shape that is independent of environmental conditions. We seek to understand this robust complexity. We are guided by two principles in the spirit of D’Arcy Thompson. First, the growth is governed by the repeated and continuous application of a fixed growth law, even as the shell evolves in overall shape, without any complex biological machinery to monitor and control the growth. Second, the growth law depends solely on local geometry at the shell’s growing edge. The first principle naturally leads to the mathematical statement that the shape of the shell is generated by the action of a Lie group on a protoconch. The second naturally leads to a particular representation of the Lie group. We use this representation to show that the shapes of nearly all known molluscan shells can be described by essentially three parameters: a scalar (scaling), a vector (orientation), and a curve (edge of the protoconch). We relate these parameters to the phylogenetic tree. In addition to the morphogenetic insight, our results potentially point to a alternative growth-based approach to engineering complex structures.

Jaeeun Sohng，Noah W. Sokol，Peter K. Weber，Steffen Schweizer，Seth Whiteaker 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04143-1

Abstract Enhanced weathering, adding crushed silicate minerals to soils, is being explored as a way to draw down atmospheric carbon dioxide, but its effects on soil organic carbon remain uncertain. We show that olivine amendments change soil carbon cycling depending on time and on labile carbon availability. In a 55-week soil microcosm experiment, adding olivine alone reduces total soil organic carbon. Adding olivine with glucose initially suppresses microbial carbon uptake, but by week 55 instead boosts microbial biomass and mineral-associated organic matter more than glucose alone, while lowering respiratory carbon loss. These late-stage gains grow stronger alongside geochemical signs of mineral weathering. Nanoscale imaging shows that newly formed soil organic carbon accumulates within pre-existing organic and mineral microsites rather than directly on olivine or newly formed clay surfaces. Our findings suggest that when labile carbon is available, enhanced weathering can increase soil carbon storage primarily through microbial processing, rather than by creating new mineral surfaces for carbon to bind to.

Abstract Nutrient enrichment and warming cause widespread costal deoxygenation, leading to hypoxia or even anoxia in bottom waters. The benthic-water-column-feedbacks that develop under persistent seasonal oxygen depletion and amplify hypoxia remain poorly constrained. A 15-month study of a seasonally hypoxic to anoxic costal system in the southwestern Baltic Sea combined water-column monitoring, sediment chemistry, metabolic rates and microbial analyses with historic data to reveal pronounced seasonal shifts in sediment processes. During winter oxygenation, organic matter degradation, fermentation, aerobic respiration, nitrate- and iron-reduction and microbial competition increased. These processes reinforced benthic feedbacks, progressively reducing the sediment’s capacity to retain sulfide over years. This weakening of sedimentary resilience exacerbates sulfide accumulation in eutrophic costal environments with growing chances of sulfidic deep-water upwelling.

This study links higher diamond grades in kimberlites to water-rich olivine. Subduction recycles surface materials into the mantle and shapes its chemical evolution. 2026-10-21

Gerardo Dominguez，Caroline Caplan，Lauren Tafla，Joshua Lucas，Ming-Chang Liu 等

DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2610806123

Mass-independent isotope fractionation (MIF) of oxygen provides key insights into atmospheric chemistry and the origin of isotopic anomalies in planetary materials. To date, MIF has been primarily associated with gas-phase processes involving symmetry-dependent reaction pathways, most notably ozone formation. Whether analogous effects can arise in condensed phases or at interfaces remains an open question. Here we report laboratory experiments demonstrating that energetic particle irradiation of icy oxide surfaces produces mass-independent oxygen isotope fractionation at temperatures as low as 10 K. Electron irradiation of SiO 2 and Al 2 O 3 substrates in the presence of H 2 O ice results in measurable shifts in Δ 17 O in the underlying solids. The magnitude of the observed anomalies demonstrates that irradiation-driven processes can generate substantial oxygen isotope heterogeneity in condensed phases. These results identify a previously unrecognized class of MIF and provide a quantitative framework for interpreting oxygen isotope signatures in radiation-processed environments, including interstellar grains and early solar system materials.

Effusive sedimentary volcanism experimentally reproduced under Martian-like conditions

2026-10-21
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Ondřej Krýza，Petr Brož，Jacob Adler，Matthew Sylvest，Manish Patel

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04155-x

Abstract Rapid boiling, cooling, and freezing are commonly assumed to rapidly quench metastable liquid eruptions exposed to near-vacuum planetary environments, influencing both sedimentary volcanism on Mars and cryovolcanism on icy worlds. However, how these coupled phase transitions govern eruptive processes remains largely unknown. Here we show experimentally that boiling instead sustains coupled ballistic-effusive mud eruptions under Martian-like pressure conditions (~4.5 mbar). Using an ice-sealed metastable reservoir, we reproduced subsurface-driven eruptions initiated by internally generated boiling-induced overpressure. Eruptions evolved systematically from short-lived ballistic fountains toward episodic effusive emplacement accompanied by coherent mud-flow formation. Ballistic transport redistributed liquid mud around the vent and promoted proximal accumulation that subsequently coalesced into sustained flows despite ongoing cooling and freezing. Numerical modeling further demonstrates that erupted mud would remain largely unfrozen during ballistic transport under Martian gravity conditions. Together, our results show that rapid boiling does not suppress sedimentary volcanism under low-pressure conditions, but instead actively sustains eruptive transport and surface emplacement. These findings provide new constraints on this process on Mars and establish a broader physical framework for metastable liquid eruptions across the Solar System.

研究结果：定年结果显示MME（1815±8 Ma和1800±9 Ma）、寄主紫苏花岗岩（1806±5 Ma）和淡色花岗岩脉（1811±8 Ma）近同期形成。MME具有卵形石英眼斑、熔蚀长石捕虏晶、较高相容元素含量和变化较大的εHf(t)（−4.2至+1.3），部分保留富集地幔亲和性；寄主紫苏花岗岩为中等、铁质、钙碱性、高钾和准铝质，负εHf(t)（−7.0至−3.2）及2551–2859 Ma模式年龄指示古老下地壳来源并混入幔源组分。淡色花岗岩εHf(t)（−7.4至−4.7）与区域古老地壳重叠，并含残余间隙紫苏辉石，说明其源自古老地壳并侵入未完全固结的紫苏花岗质晶粥。这些结果将富集岩石圈地幔再充填与古老地壳再造联系到约1.8 Ga跨华北造山带后碰撞热松弛和伸展作用。

局限性：仅依据摘要难以评估数据质量、样品代表性和年代学解释的稳健性；MME成因、岩浆混合程度及淡色花岗岩与寄主岩石的精确关系仍需完整岩相学、地球化学和同位素数据支撑。后碰撞伸展构造背景的判定也可能受区域构造年代学框架和地球化学判别图解多解性的影响。

未来方向：后续可结合锆石Hf-O同位素、全岩Sr-Nd同位素、相平衡模拟及区域岩浆-变质年代学对比，进一步限定壳幔混合过程、岩浆房演化及晚古元古代后碰撞伸展的时空范围。

研究相关性：该论文对研究华北克拉通古元古代构造演化、后碰撞岩浆作用、紫苏花岗岩成因、MME与岩浆混合以及古老地壳再造的研究者具有直接参考价值，也可为理解壳幔相互作用与伸展背景下花岗质岩浆系统演化提供案例。
