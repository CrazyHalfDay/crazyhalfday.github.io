Hierarchical self-organization of framboidal pyrite enables biosignature preservation

2026-09-03
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Jing Gao，Wanghua Wu，Dongmei Wang，Yue Wang

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04025-6

Microscopic and spectroscopic observations of framboidal pyrite from the Gakkel Ridge support a 3-stage growth model, which is compatible with, but does not require, biological activity and can explain the preservation of biosignatures in framboidal structures Framboidal pyrite serves as a critical archive of mineralization and paleoenvironmental history, yet its formation remains contested between biological templating and abiotic self-organization. Integrating geometric and crystallographic analyses of framboidal pyrite from the Gakkel Ridge, we reveal a hierarchical architecture defined by fractal geometry (fractal dimensions of 1.7–1.9) and systematic core-to-rim gradients in crystallinity and lattice strain. These findings underpin a unified three-stage growth model: initiation by synchronous nucleation, advancement via diffusion-limited aggregation into a fractal skeleton, and finalization through surface-energy minimization driving spheroidization. This mechanism provides a universal abiotic explanation for framboidal formation across diverse mineral systems. While biological activity may provide the initial supersaturation pulse, the characteristic texture remains a robust outcome of physical forces. The resulting architecture optimizes biosignature preservation, wherein defect-rich cores adsorb organic matter encapsulated by high-crystallinity rims. These findings resolve the long-standing biotic-abiotic dichotomy, establishing framboids as physically inevitable products and robust geochemical proxies for decoding mineralogical biosignatures in both terrestrial and extraterrestrial contexts.

Global ecosystem carbon losses from earthquakes

地震造成的全球生态系统碳损失

2026-09-03
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Wen He，Wen Zeng，Yu Zhan，Deliang Chen，Tien Ming Lee 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-04012-x

Abstract Earthquakes disrupt terrestrial ecosystem carbon storage by triggering landslides and altering land cover, yet a global quantification of these carbon losses has been lacking. Here we quantify the global earthquake-induced carbon losses by combining vegetation models, land cover, carbon density, and earthquake records. Focusing on strong earthquakes worldwide between 2006 and 2018, we find that these events cause a cumulative loss of 38.86 ± 6.23 teragrams of carbon by 2022. Forest ecosystems require at least six years to begin recovery. These carbon losses are unevenly distributed across countries, with 84.6% of affected countries being emerging and developing economies. In Nepal and Ecuador, cumulative earthquake-induced carbon losses exceed half of their cumulative land-use change emissions during the post-earthquake period. Our study establishes a global benchmark for carbon losses from geophysical disturbances, offering a crucial reference for national carbon accounting and disaster resilience strategies.

Distinct constraints but shared dynamics shape the macroevolution of volatile organic compound chemodiversity

不同的约束条件与共享的动力学机制塑造了挥发性有机化合物化学多样性的宏观演化

2026-09-03
Nature Communications (IF: 14.7)

Ziyue Xu，Jiadong Zhu，Xu Liu，Longjun Wu

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-77351-0

Organisms produce volatile organic compounds for communication, defense and environmental responses, but how this chemodiversity evolved is unclear. This study shows that in gymnosperms, chemodiversity shows weak covariation, core compounds are highly integrated, and both evolve in a punctuated mode. Complex metabolic systems have evolved across the tree of life, producing remarkable chemodiversity, yet the structure and evolutionary modes underlying this diversity remain poorly understood. Here, we investigate the macroevolution of chemodiversity using a comprehensive dataset of gymnosperm foliage volatile organic compounds (VOCs) collected from the literature. We find that chemodiversity is dominated by a small fraction of “core” compounds. Although few in number, these core VOCs are widespread and abundant. To understand the full picture, we investigate the macroevolution of both high-dimensional global chemodiversity, and the dominant core chemodiversity. We find that both global and core chemodiversity have evolved through similar dynamics: punctuated evolution rather than gradual drift. We also identify a dichotomy in constraint, where global chemodiversity exhibits weak covariance—reflecting high evolutionary lability—and the core chemodiversity is characterized by high covariance and strong integration. We perform ancestral state reconstruction and find that this complex system is not a recent innovation; the major, core VOCs were already established in ancestral lineages prior to the Carboniferous period. These findings not only reveal the hierarchical structure of the gymnosperm VOC system, with its underlying constraints and dynamics, but also provide a framework for exploring the structure and evolutionary dynamics of chemodiversity.

Paleogeographic controls on Phanerozoic weathering rates and implications for climate regulation

古地理对显生宙风化速率的控制及其对气候调节的影响

2026-09-02
Nature Communications (IF: 14.7)

R. J. Graham，B. B. Cael，M. Fu，D. S. Abbot

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-77289-3

Abstract Earth’s climate has remained habitable for billions of years, a stability usually attributed to a negative feedback from silicate weathering – the chemical breakdown of silicate rocks on land, which consumes atmospheric carbon dioxide faster as climate warms. However, whether temperature or hydrology primarily drives this feedback remains uncertain. Here we show the two controls leave geographic fingerprints: in an ensemble of climate simulations with randomized continents, temperature-controlled weathering depends on tropical land fraction, whereas hydrology-controlled weathering depends on continental fragmentation. Stronger weathering removes more carbon dioxide, cooling the planet, so each regime predicts anti-correlation between temperature and its controlling geography. Reconstructed global temperatures spanning 480 million years are anti-correlated with tropical land fraction, except from roughly 295 to 200 million years ago – overlapping the supercontinent Pangea – when they anti-correlate with continental fragmentation. The mechanism stabilizing Earth’s climate may thus have evolved, with temperature control apparently dominant for most of this period.

Sezim E. Güvercin，Sylvain Barbot，Lei Zhang，Mehmet Köküm，Zekang Yang 等

DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aef6076

Crustal faults across geological settings are often enriched in phyllosilicates because of the hydrothermal alteration of wall rocks. The frictional behavior of these altered rocks at mid-crustal conditions remains poorly understood. Here, we show that mica-schist gouge under crustal hydrothermal conditions can host mixed behavior promoting shallow aseismic creep and confined seismic ruptures. We conduct triaxial deformation experiments on natural samples from the East Anatolian Fault, Turkey, and simulate the resulting dynamics on the Pütürge segment during seismic cycles using a physical model of rock friction and a realistic thermal structure. The frictional properties of mica schist are compatible with the 2020 moment magnitude ( M w ) 6.8 Elazığ earthquake and the northern rupture arrest of the 2023 M w 7.8 Kahramanmaraş event, illustrating how the thermal and lithological structure affects rupture style and segmentation.

The 2022 Hunga volcano eruption generated one of the largest and most destructive volcanic tsunamis ever recorded. Moon’s farside near-zero paleomagnetic field suggests a global lunar dynamo cessation

月球背面近零古磁场暗示月球发电机整体停转

2026-09-02
Science Advances (IF: 11.7)

Xuelong Jiang，Fei Su，Huapei Wang，Runchuan Liu，Ting Cao 等

DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.adz6195

Recent paleomagnetic studies of Apollo and Chang’e returned samples suggest that the lunar dynamo was active from ∼4.2 billion years ago (Ga) and ceased by ∼1.0 Ga. However, uncertainties remain regarding the temporal and spatial distribution of the lunar paleomagnetic field, as almost all of the returned samples are from the nearside, except for those returned by Chang’e-6. Here, we present the first paleomagnetic analysis of a lunar meteorite, Northwest Africa 15528, which is a feldspathic regolith breccia likely from the farside of the Moon. Our results not only indicate that it recorded a near-zero magnetic field (below a few hundred nanoteslas) at ∼0.7 Ga but also show that lunar meteorites can provide critical paleomagnetic information. Combined with two young Apollo samples from the nearside, Apollo 15015 (formed at ∼0.9 Ga) and Apollo 15465 (formed at ∼0.5 Ga), our findings suggest the absence of a global dynamo-generated lunar magnetic field for the past one billion years.

Topographic stress controls fissure segmentation and eruptive cone formation during the Laki and Eldgjá eruptions

2026-09-02
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Maria Hurley，Francesco Maccaferri，Thomas R. Walter

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03980-4

Abstract Elongated dykes in tectonic rifts feed extensive basaltic fissure eruptions, yet surface eruption sites are highly discontinuous and unpredictable. Here we show that along-strike topographic variations, and the associated stress gradients, correlate with this localisation. We investigate eruptive cones at the two most productive historical basaltic eruptions in Iceland, Laki (1783–1784) and Eldgjá (~939–940). Morphological analyses of > 300 cones reveal volume variations (10 0 –10 7 m 3 ) and segmentation along ~ 100 km of fissures. Comparing cone distribution and volumes along the rift zone with pre-eruptive stress models, we demonstrate that high topographic stress gradients inhibit cone growth. We identify a critical stress gradient threshold predicting where larger cones are inhibited, while horizontal stress gradient peaks predict the location of fissure gaps. Our findings establish an empirical and physical link between topographic stress and volcanic morphologies, complementing previous models with a quantitative framework that aims at improving the forecasting of along-rift vent locations and development.

Abstract The deep continental biosphere hosts microbial communities employing ancient metabolisms such as methanogenesis. The log-logistic evolution of meteorite friability and implications for asteroid regolith

陨石易碎性的对数逻辑斯蒂演化及其对小行星风化层的意义

2026-09-01
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Suraj Bhatt，Laurence A. J. Garvie，Desireé Cotto-Figueroa，Erik Asphaug，Christian G. Hoover

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03494-z

Friability of meteorites, the tendency to crumble under repeated low stresses, influences the collisional and geological evolution of asteroids and small planetary bodies. Here, we quantify meteorite friability through laboratory tumbling experiments on a broad range of stony near-Earth objects. Disaggregation follows a bounded multiplicative process: it first accelerates with cumulative impacts, then slows as breakable material is exhausted. A log-logistic model describes this behavior and captures a material-dependent limit due to the “pillow-effect,” where accumulating fine debris cushions further impacts. The number of rotations needed for complete disaggregation varies by more than six orders of magnitude, indicating much broader timescales for regolith maturation and rubble-pile evolution than previously assumed. Fine dust generated from friable materials may also enhance cohesion under microgravity, helping explain the weak yet cohesive surfaces of rubble-pile asteroids such as Bennu and Ryugu. These measurements provide a framework for quantitatively incorporating friability into asteroid evolution models. Asteroid regolith maturation and rubble-pile evolution occur over comparably broad timescales, according to foundational friability measurements derived from mechanical tumbling experiments conducted on a broad range of meteorite types

The crust–mantle interactions and geodynamic regimes of late Archean continental crust formation and evolution are a hot topic in global Precambrian research. The Yudongzi Complex is a typical Archean basement unit in the northwestern Yangtze Craton and features Neoarchean tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite (TTG) gneisses, K-rich granitoids and metavolcanic rocks. According to the lithological features, magmatic zircon ages and whole-rock geochemical characteristics, four main lithological assemblages are identified. (1) The ca. 2.67–2.64 Ga diorites, quartz diorites and granodiorites feature high Mg# (47–60) values, Sr + Ba (average 1480 ppm) contents and K2O/Na2O (0.36–1.06) ratios, which are typical of Archean sanukitoids. They have high zircon δ18O (5.61 ‰ to 7.26 ‰) values and positive zircon εHf(t) (+1.6 to +7.4) and whole-rock εNd(t) (+0.2 and +3.4) values and were formed by the partial melting of lithospheric mantle metasomatized by subduction-related fluids and sediment and oceanic slab melts. (2) The ca. 2.68 Ga TTGs are characterized by high Mg# (48–54) values and high Cr (9.19–29.07 ppm) and Ni (6.81–18.57 ppm) contents and are most likely derived from the partial melting of oceanic slabs contaminated by 1–10 % mantle peridotite. The ca. 2.51 Ga TTGs have lower Mg# values and MgO contents and are derived from the partial melting of juvenile mafic lower crust. (3) The ca. 2.50 Ga monzogranites display geochemical affinities to S-type granites and were generated by partial melting of ancient psammites. (4) The ca. 2.53 G a mafic rocks can be subdivided into arc-type rocks and N-MORB-type rocks on the basis of their geochemical characteristics. The N-MORB-type rocks were formed by interactions between asthenospheric-derived melts and the overlying metasomatized lithospheric mantle. Considering the petrogenesis of the diverse lithological assemblages, we propose that the ca. 2.7–2.64 Ga igneous rocks and ca. 2.53–2.50 Ga rocks most likely formed in an oceanic slab subduction setting and a back-arc basin setting, respectively. The partial melting P–T conditions of the studied ca. 2.68 Ga and ca. 2.51 Ga slab-derived TTG magmas are estimated to have involved high pressures and temperatures. The geothermal gradient in the northwestern Yangtze Craton is calculated to have been ∼18–19 °C/km and ∼19–20 °C/km in the subduction belt at ca. 2.68 Ga and ca. 2.51 Ga, indicating a possible Neoarchaean warm subduction system in the Yangtze Craton.

研究背景：该研究旨在揭示火山成因块状硫化物（VMS）矿床中闪锌矿对关键金属（Ga、Ge、In、Se等）的富集规律及其地质控制因素。以往对VMS系统中闪锌矿内这些元素的分布和形成机制认识不足，因此选择新疆小热泉子Cu-Zn矿床作为典型案例，通过系统研究其多阶段闪锌矿的地球化学特征，以深化对关键金属在VMS及类似热液系统中富集过程的理解。

研究结果：研究发现I号矿带的Sph-E1和Sph-E2闪锌矿具有高Fe（最高14.9 wt%）和高Se（最高1431 ppm）含量，其中Se以固溶体或方铅矿微包裹体形式存在；Sph-E2还显著富集In（最高158 ppm）、Ag、Sb和Sn，其微量元素进入受Ag+ + Sn2+ + In3+ ↔ 3Zn2+ 和 Ag+ + Sb3+ ↔ 2Zn2+ 替代机制控制。III号矿带的闪锌矿则相对贫Fe（平均5.4 wt%）和Se（平均66 ppm）。综合闪锌矿化学特征、与黄铜矿的交代关系及GGIMFis温度计，表明高Se的Sph-E1和Sph-E2形成于高温（282–329°C）、富Se的热液流体，该流体可能受岩浆挥发分或流体贡献。所有闪锌矿均显示低Ga、Ge、In的特征，可能源于源区这些元素的亏损。

局限性：研究的局限性包括：仅对一个矿床的两个矿带进行了分析，样品代表性可能有限；对微量元素赋存状态的解释部分依赖微米级包裹体观察，可能遗漏纳米级或晶格中更隐蔽的赋存形式；GGIMFis温度计的应用前提和误差范围未充分讨论，且高温流体来源的推断缺乏直接的稳定同位素或流体包裹体证据支持。

未来方向：未来可结合原位S-Pb同位素和流体包裹体微区分析，进一步约束成矿流体的来源与演化路径；并对VMS系统中闪锌矿的微量元素分配行为开展实验模拟，以量化温度、压力和流体组成对关键金属富集的控制机制。

研究相关性：该论文为研究热液矿床中关键金属（特别是In、Se、Ga、Ge）的分布规律和富集机制提供了翔实的案例数据，其方法学（如闪锌矿精细分类、LA-ICP-MS元素图谱与替代机制解析）和地质解释框架（多阶段流体演化与岩浆贡献）对从事VMS矿床、关键金属成矿作用以及矿物微量元素示踪研究的科研人员具有直接参考价值。
