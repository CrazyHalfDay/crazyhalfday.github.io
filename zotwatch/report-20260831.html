A dual-mineral protection mechanism for organic matter preservation and its implications for Mars habitability and biosignature detection

有机质保存的双矿物保护机制及其对火星宜居性和生物特征检测的意义

2026-08-29
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Tongtong Huang，Jianxun Shen，Li Liu，Jialong Hao，Yan Chen 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03992-0

Understanding organic matter preservation in extreme environments is crucial for detecting potential biosignatures on Mars. Although clay minerals and evaporites can each preserve organic matter individually, their synergistic preservation mechanisms remain an active area of investigation. Here, we investigate the preservation of organic matter in ancient evaporite minerals from the Qaidam Basin, using integrated in situ microanalytical techniques to analyze solid/fluid inclusions within displacive gypsum and cumulate halite formed during the Middle Pleistocene. Our analyses reveal well-preserved organic matter, including β-carotene and kerogen, adsorbed onto clay particles both in solid inclusions within gypsum and fluid inclusions within halite. Geochemical data are consistent with a potential biogenic contribution, whereas metagenomic data from modern Qaidam soils provide ecological context rather than direct evidence for the origin of the preserved organic matter. We propose a dual-mineral protection mechanism, wherein clay minerals initially adsorb organic compounds, which are subsequently encapsulated by growing salt crystals. This two-stage process leverages the complementary protective properties of both mineral types and enhances long-term organic preservation. Our findings identify a specific mineral association capable of preserving Martian organic biosignatures and provide strategic guidance for selecting exploration targets in future Mars missions. A two-step interaction between clay minerals and evaporite salts may promote organic matter preservation, with possible relevance to Mars, according to microanalytical analysis of the Middle Pleistocene gypsum and halite minerals from China’s western Qaidam Basin.

Metasomatic dehydration of talc constrains the maximum depth of slab–mantle decoupling in subduction zones

滑石的交代脱水限制了俯冲带板片-地幔解耦的最大深度

2026-08-29
Communications Earth &amp; Environment (IF: 8.1)

Nanfei Cheng，I-Ming Chou，Zhenhao Duan，Zhou Gui，Haiyan Zhang 等

DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03979-x

Abstract Talc largely contributes to water transport into Earth’s interior in subduction zones and lubrication of the slab–mantle interface. However, its maximum stability limit has typically been evaluated under silica-saturated conditions. In-situ high-pressure, high-temperature experiments up to 33.8 kbar and 875 °C reveal that in water-rich, low-silica environments, geologically evidenced by talc coexisting with antigorite and olivine at natural subduction interfaces, talc undergoes a metasomatic dehydration reaction that produces forsterite and a silica-bearing aqueous fluid. This reaction shifts the dehydration boundary to ≈ 705 °C at 10 kbar and ≈ 650 °C at 30 kbar in the experimental low-silica condition, that is 90–170 °C lower than that under silica-saturated conditions. Critically, this metasomatic boundary intersects slab thermal profiles at a depth of ≈ 80 km across diverse global subduction zones, from cold (e.g., Mariana) to hot (e.g., Cascadia), matching the maximum depth of decoupling between the subducting slab and mantle wedge, which separates the stagnant, cold forearc mantle from the flowing, hot arc mantle.

Nature Communications (IF: 14.7)

Lewis J. Alcott，Benjamin J. W. Mills，Andrey Bekker，Zidong Peng，Simon W. Poulton

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-76597-y

Abstract Earth’s first rise in atmospheric oxygen occurred over a protracted period from ca. 2.43 to 2.06 billion years ago during the Great Oxidation Episode (GOE). The GOE was marked by extreme climatic instability, which likely impacted the bioavailability of the principal limiting nutrient, phosphorus. Here, we investigate phosphorus cycling across the final two glaciations of the GOE, as documented by the Transvaal Supergroup, South Africa. Utilising a phosphorus phase partitioning approach, we show that following an enhanced weathering-induced phosphorus influx that stimulated oxygenation in the aftermath of glaciation, sedimentary phosphorus recycling was controlled by feedback-driven fluctuations in sulphate availability and oceanic redox state. Biogeochemical modelling suggests that this dynamic interplay drove major instability in atmospheric oxygen levels during Earth’s protracted transition to a persistently oxygenated atmosphere.

Foreshock productivity and rupture nucleation in injection-induced earthquakes in western Canada

2026-08-27
Science (IF: 44.7)

Bei Wang，Honn Kao，Hongyu Yu，Yan Jiang，Qiang Lin 等

DOI: 10.1126/science.aed9893

Foreshock activity is a key consideration in traffic-light protocol (TLP) to mitigate seismic risk from injection-induced earthquakes (IIEs). However, the seismogenic processes of IIE foreshocks remain poorly understood. Using an enhanced western Canada catalog (2014–2024), we analyzed 77 IIE sequences to statistically delineate foreshock patterns before local magnitude ( M L ) ≥ 3 mainshocks. We found that 92% are preceded by foreshocks and that foreshock productivity and spatiotemporal patterns reflect the interplay among fluid injection, the seismogenic index, and the fault stress state. Sequence-specific analyses elucidate three nucleation models: fluid-driven preslip with weakened source asperity, fluid-driven preslip with intact source asperity, and fluid-driven cascade, highlighting the central role of fluids in enabling aseismic slip and interevent stress transfer before mainshock rupture. These results imply that IIE monitoring strategies should be spatially conditioned according to foreshock productivity.

Reversed marine phosphorus gradient in the Ediacaran Ocean

埃迪卡拉纪海洋中反转的海洋磷梯度

2026-08-27
Nature Communications (IF: 14.7)

Matthew S. Dodd，Chao Li，Meng Cheng，Jun Hu，Zihu Zhang 等

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-77093-z

Carbonate associated phosphate indicates that Ediacaran oceans had more P in shallow waters but less in the deep sea, opposite to modern. A distal P shortage may have aided deep-water oxygenation and early animals. The Ediacaran Period (635–539 Ma) witnessed the emergence of early animals, rapid oceanic oxygenation, and rising oceanic P availability. However, the relationships among these developments remain intensely debated. Here, we present high-resolution carbonate-associated phosphate (CAP) data from seven Ediacaran sections spanning diverse depositional environments across South China. Contrary to the modern ocean, our measurements reveal an inverted phosphorus (P) gradient, with lower P availability at depth. By integrating these data with a quantitative biogeochemical model, we show that increasing shallow-water P inputs drove enhanced productivity and bottom-water anoxia on continental shelves, while P-starved distal oceans became progressively oxygenated from the top downward, providing an oxygenated niche—a deep-ocean cradle—for early metazoan evolution. Our study establishes a conceptual framework linking nutrient dynamics, marine oxygenation, and evolutionary innovation in Earth’s early history.

Nickel isotopes (δ 60 Ni) are an emerging tool for understanding ocean biogeochemical evolution, given improved modern Ni budget constraints. Here, we provide constraints on past ocean Ni isotope compositions from organic-rich sediments from Tarfaya Basin core SN°4. The data constrain Late Cretaceous ocean δ 60 Ni to ∼+0.8 to +1.0 per mil (‰), lower than the modern value of +1.34‰. Tidal tomography reveals a thermal anomaly beneath Mars’s crustal dichotomy

2026-08-26
Nature (IF: 64.8)

Alexander Berne，Nicholas Wagner，Isamu Matsuyama，Sander Goossens，Antonio Genova 等

DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10893-x

Abstract Mars undergoes seasonal tidal forcing as a result of its eccentric orbit and the tilt of its rotation axis relative to the Sun 1 . Response to this forcing produces temporal variations in the Martian gravity field and is sensitive to the internal structure of the planet 2–4 . Using tracking data from the Mars Global Surveyor (MGS), Mars Odyssey (ODY) and Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) spacecraft 5–7 , we demonstrate that degree-3 components of the time-variable gravity field of Mars differ by up to 300% from predictions for a spherically symmetric planet 8–10 . These deviations can be explained if the effective shear modulus of the mantle varies by >20% over a roughly north–south pattern that closely aligns with the surface expression of the Martian crustal dichotomy 11 . On the basis of this correlation, we infer preservation of a 200–400 °C thermal anomaly in the present-day mantle below the southern highlands of Mars. This temperature variation could reflect regional mantle convection 12,13 or insulation by the thick southern highlands crust of Mars that has persisted over several billion years 14,15 .

Slab fluid-driven oxidation of Archean sub-arc mantle: A potential trigger for the great oxidation event

板片流体驱动的太古宙弧下地幔氧化：大氧化事件的一个潜在触发因素

2026-08-28
Earth and Planetary Science Letters (IF: 4.8)

Kai Wang，Jiashun Hu，Richard M. Palin，Keda Cai，Xiangsong Wang

DOI: 10.1016/j.epsl.2026.120302

A redox disparity between Archean modified and ambient mantle, analogous to that between modern arc and mid-ocean ridge (MOR) mantle, hints at the operation of subduction on the early Earth; however, the geochemical agents responsible for Archean mantle oxidation remain ambiguous. Here we assess the redox capacity of volatiles released from Archean hydrothermally altered oceanic crust (AOC) using combined geodynamic and thermodynamic modeling. We show that the slab-top thermal structure governs the depth and extent of AOC dehydration, whereas the bulk-rock ferric-to-total iron ratio (XFe3+) modulates the speciation and concentration of carbon and sulfur in coexisting aqueous fluids. Reduced AOC predominantly mobilizes carbon and sulfur during late-stage dehydration, while oxidized AOC promotes earlier sulfur release and generates sulfate-rich fluids. The migration and infiltration of these carbon- and sulfur-bearing fluids can oxidize the Archean sub-arc mantle by 2–5 orders of magnitude, facilitating the generation of oxidized arc magmas. We suggest that the intensification of arc volcanism, related to the establishment of a more globally connected subduction network toward the end of the Archean, increased the flux of relatively oxidized gases into the atmosphere, enabling oxygen sources to outpace sinks and acting as a trigger for the Great Oxidation Event (GOE).

Magmatic Volatile Controls on Cu and Mo Mineralization in Collisional Porphyry Systems

2026-08-28
Economic Geology (IF: 3.2)

Yingcai Sun，Rui Wang，Qiushi Zhou，Li Tang

DOI: 10.5382/econgeo.5257

Abstract Porphyry deposits represent globally significant sources of Cu and Mo, yet the magmatic volatile controls on Cu versus Mo metallogenic divergence remain poorly quantified in collisional settings. Here, we compare porphyry Cu systems from the Gangdese belt, southern Tibet, with porphyry Mo systems from the southern margin of the North China craton—two collisional belts with contrasting magma sources and metal endowments. By integrating apatite geochemistry with thermodynamic forward modeling, we reconstructed volatile evolution during ore-forming magma differentiation. Copper-forming magmas were more hydrous (initial H2O contents of ~5–6 wt %) than Mo-forming magmas (~3.8–4.7 wt %), whereas both were moderately oxidized. Modeled fluid-saturation thresholds are ~11 wt % H2O for Cu systems (~0.5 GPa) and ~9.2 wt % H2O for Mo systems (~0.4 GPa). Copper-related magmas were initially enriched in Cl (0.25–0.35 wt %) and F (0.40–0.55 wt %) but underwent significant F depletion (bulk DFc/m≈1.8) driven by early, extensive amphibole fractionation. In contrast, Mo-related magmas had lower initial F (0.05–0.25 wt %) and comparable Cl (0.20–0.35 wt %) but became progressively enriched in both halogens (DFc/m≈0.7; DClc/m≈0.14) during prolonged crystallization with limited amphibole crystallization. This contrasting H2O-F-Cl evolution links source-imposed volatile budgets to Cu-dominated outcomes versus Mo-dominated outcomes: Cl-enriched residual melts favor Cu-transporting fluids, whereas late-stage F enrichment likely lowers melt viscosity, prolongs differentiation, and promotes Mo enrichment in evolved residual melts.

DOI: 10.1029/2026jb034130

Abstract The mobility of aqueous fluids in subducting slabs fundamentally controls volatile and element cycling, yet the efficiency of fluid extraction from eclogitized oceanic crust remains poorly constrained. While NaCl is a key solute in subduction zone fluids, its specific role in controlling fluid connectivity in slab eclogite has not been quantified. Here, we present systematic measurements of dihedral angles ( θ ) in the eclogite–NaCl–H 2 O system at 2–15 GPa and 800–1200°C. The addition of NaCl reduces garnet–fluid–garnet, garnet–fluid–clinopyroxene, and clinopyroxene–fluid–clinopyroxene θ below the 60° interconnectivity threshold under all investigated conditions. Notably, fluid connectivity ( θ θ ≈ 0°) occurs at pressures ≥9 GPa and 1200°C. These results demonstrate that saline fluids form interconnected networks within eclogitized slabs, enabling efficient and pervasive fluid release in global subduction zones. This mechanism provides a direct mechanism for continuous element fluxing, metasomatism, and flux melting in the mantle wedge. Consequently, our findings imply that subducting oceanic crust is unlikely to transport significant free water to the deep mantle; instead, the deep mantle water inventory may be governed by water stored in hydrous and/or nominally anhydrous minerals.

Carbon is crucial to the genesis of carbonatites and alkaline rocks associated with rare metal deposits, yet its origin remains poorly constrained. Here we present an integrated study of petrography, titanite U-Pb geochronology, and whole-rock geochemistry (major and trace elements, Sr-Nd-Zn isotopes) to evaluate the role of recycled sedimentary carbonate in the generation of carbonatite-syenite complex from the Miaoya Nb-REE deposit and the alkaline volcanic rocks from the Yehuzhai Nb deposit, which are spatially related in the South Qinling (SQ) region. The combined titanite U-Pb ages, major and trace elements, and Sr-Nd isotopes indicate that these carbonatites and alkaline rocks were derived from a mantle metasomatized by oceanic crust-derived melts/fluids and formed in an early Paleozoic extensional tectonic setting linked to the opening of the Mianlue Ocean. In contrast, the Yehuzhai alkaline rocks yield MORB-like δ66Zn values (0.29–0.36‰), which can be explained by partial melting of peridotitic mantle with negligible contribution from sedimentary carbonate. The contrasting Zn isotopic signatures reflect the varying degree of recycled carbonate contribution to the mantle heterogeneity beneath the SQ region. Collectively, these findings highlight a genetic relationship among deep carbon cycle, tectonic activity, carbonatite-alkaline rocks generation, and rare metal mineralization.
